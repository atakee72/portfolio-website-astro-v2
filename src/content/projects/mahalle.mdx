## Problem

The Schillerkiez (Berlin Neukölln) needed a digital commons — forum,
calendar, marketplace, local news, air-quality data — without surrendering
to a global platform.

## Build

Astro SSR + React + Svelte islands, MongoDB direct driver, auth-astro
with JWT, TanStack Query with 24h localStorage persistence, Motion for
animation, Resend for transactional email, Sentry for observability.
Forum, calendar, marketplace, newsboard with daily fetch, recommendations,
reports & admin moderation — plus a full MDX blog ("Die Beilage") and a
profile & account-lifecycle system: an own-profile Meldebogen with a
Kiez-Chronik tenure timeline, a printable QR-coded Steckbrief card,
public neighbor profiles, self-service email/password change, and a
7-day-undo account deletion that anonymizes into an "Ehemaliges
Mitglied" tombstone while preserving authored content.

## Architecture

Multilingual AI moderation (TR/EN/DE + leetspeak filters) gates all
user-generated content before it lands, backed by a 3-strike ban system
that drops banned sessions to read-only across the write surface and
walks admins through a "Ban-Bremse" confirmation step before a
third-strike rejection. The old dark-glass design system has been fully
replaced by a "kiosk" aesthetic — paper-warm surfaces, ink borders,
carved-italic titles — across every page, forum to blog, opened by a
one-per-session splash intro that skips on reduced-motion. BLUME
air-quality proxy and Schillerkiez demographics still surface from
public APIs. The marketplace has matured into a 13-category kiosk
taxonomy with draft/publish, a 21-day listing-visibility window, and
Resend-relayed buyer↔seller contact that never exposes an email address.

## Reflection

Built with a manifesto attached — the project's moral charter sits at
the root of the repo as `MANIFESTO.md`, now paired with a PolyForm
Noncommercial license and a custom field-of-use rider that excludes
political parties, for-profit deployment, and resident-surveillance use
cases.
