## Problem

A light artist needed a portfolio that handled image-heavy galleries,
flexible block content, and an artist statement that could change
language and tone across exhibitions — without learning a CMS.

## Build

Astro static site fed by Sanity Studio. Portable Text renders artist
statements with mixed-media blocks (image + text + video). Sanity's
image pipeline handles crop variants for hero vs gallery vs thumb.

## Architecture

The Studio runs locally for the artist's edits, deploys via `sanity
deploy`. Astro pulls from the Sanity Content Lake at build time and
emits a fully-static site to Vercel.

## Reflection

Sanity's Portable Text was the unlock — letting the artist mix image,
video, and prose inline, without forcing every page into a template.
The cost is two deploys to think about (Studio + Astro), and a brief
training session on what a "block" is. Worth it for the editorial range.
