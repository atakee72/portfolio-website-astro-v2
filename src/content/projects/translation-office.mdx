## Problem

A translation office was losing after-hours leads to voicemail. They
needed a phone-facing agent that could intake job descriptions, quote
turnaround, and book follow-ups in their CRM.

## Build

TypeScript + Gemini API, scaffolded in Google AI Studio. The agent
handles intake in EN/DE/TR, classifies job type (document, certified,
interpreting), and hands off to a human when the request leaves its
trained domain.

## Architecture

WIP. Gemini handles ASR + dialogue; structured outputs feed back into
the CRM via webhook. The hard part is graceful handoff — knowing when
to stop talking and route to a human.

## Reflection

Voice agents fail differently from text agents — silence reads as broken,
even when the model is just thinking. Confidence thresholds and explicit
handoff phrases ("let me transfer you") matter more than prompt quality.
The next iteration will surface call duration as a quality signal.
